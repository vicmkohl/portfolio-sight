About

VIC KOHL

I make images that hold still long enough to say something, and move when they need to.

Based in Portland, I've spent the last eight years directing and shooting music films, commercial work, and documentary shorts for clients who care about the frame as much as the story. My work leans toward natural light, patient camera movement, and sound design that does half the storytelling.
