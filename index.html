About

Alex Rivera

I make images that hold still long enough to say something, and move when they need to.

Based in Los Angeles, I've spent the last eight years directing and shooting music films, commercial work, and documentary shorts for clients who care about the frame as much as the story. My work leans toward natural light, patient camera movement, and sound design that does half the storytelling.

Previously on set for Brand One, Label Two, and Studio Three. Selected by Publication Name as one to watch, 2025.
